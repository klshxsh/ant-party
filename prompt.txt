Let's write a web page app for learning maths.
Start with questions from times tables but also to include simple add/subtract/divide.

To reward progress, there is a theme involving an ant party.
After each question you see a cute ant walk past on its way to the party.
After N questions you can choose an item of food for the ant party.
After M questions you see the ants (the same ones that walked past) with the food, enjoying the party.

Ask me questions and we will put a plan together.
